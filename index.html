See Kruvex In Action

A powerful dashboard designed for efficient crew management and job planning

Upload a single Excel spreadsheet and instantly see all your jobs plotted on the map. Kruvex automatically places job markers for every address, or add jobs manually one at a time.

Every job from your spreadsheet becomes an organized job card. Each card contains all the details you need—location, work orders, ticket numbers, and custom checklists.

Build your workforce by creating crews and assigning team members. Organize your entire operation with custom crew types and team structures.

See your crews' workload at a glance and assign work responsibly. Balance job distribution to keep teams productive without overloading anyone.

Keep track of incomplete, assigned, and completed jobs. See real-time status updates and monitor progress across your entire operation.

Generate professional PDF reports and documentation. Export job details, checklists, and crew assignments for records and communication.

Create custom checklists and turn all your paper checklists into digital. Build reusable templates that ensure crews complete work consistently every time.

Track checklist completion across all jobs. See which crews are following procedures and ensure quality standards are met every time.

Use the calendar to schedule jobs weeks or months in advance. Plan your workload strategically and keep crews booked with future work.

View analytics and insights about your jobs. Track performance metrics, completion rates, and crew productivity to make data-driven decisions.

Optimize crew routes to minimize travel time and maximize productivity. Plan efficient job sequences based on location and crew capacity.

Customize your workspace with comprehensive settings including employee management, batch imports, and utility provider configurations.
